- **Stamped:** only `claim-integrity-design.md`. Its stamp's blob is `git rev-parse HEAD:docs/initiatives/autonomous-ready-issue-loop/claim-integrity-design.md`. The stamp is written last, after the clean round. Any later edit to the design needs a new round and a new stamp, inside the cap.
- **Reviewed, not stamped:** the [PRD](../../prds/autonomous-ready-issue-loop.md), [ADR-0045](../../adr/0045-lair-software-factory.md) and the [solution design](solution-design.md).
- **Exit:** the review exits only when zero critical or high findings are open and one round raises no new findings. It is capped at 3 rounds; the user raised the cap to 4 for this review after round 3, under Record keys.
- **Passes:** round 1 ran two fresh read-only `hivemind:cerebrate` analysis passes in parallel, each with "Memory context: none".
  - Pass A covered code truth, governance and ADR conflicts, and existing-path coverage.
  - Pass B covered failure modes, trust and injection, and testability.
- From round 2, under the user's procedure change of 2026-11-21 in Record keys, each round runs one fresh read-only `hivemind:cerebrate` analysis pass covering both angles, delegated with effort high. This design touches trust and credentials, so each round also runs one pass through the configured local review tool via `run-review.sh`, or records its skip when no tool is configured. The confirming round reviews only the diff since the previous round and the fixes to open findings, with effort medium.
- **Finding IDs:** new finding IDs take the prefix `C`, then the round.
  - Round 1: then the pass and the finding's number in that pass. `C1-A2` is round 1, pass A, finding 2.
  - From round 2: the cerebrate pass's findings take the finding's number, `C<round>-<n>`, and the local-tool pass's take `T` and the number, `C<round>-T<n>`. `C2-4` is round 2, cerebrate finding 4; `C2-T1` is round 2, tool finding 1.

Each later review round adds its own section after Inherited findings.

## Dispositions

Every finding is open until it gets exactly one disposition:

- **fixed**: the record says where.
- **deferred**: the record names a tracked issue.
- **recorded**: the record says where.

**Open** is a state, not a disposition. An open finding is counted in `open_counts`, and its disposition column reads `open`.
